# Product

## Register

product

## Users

SillyBunny users editing character cards, lorebooks, and presets who need to inspect and recover earlier versions after mistakes, extension updates, or silent writes.

## Product Purpose

Provide dependable local history for data SillyBunny does not back up. Success means users can see what changed, restore deliberately, and retain a verified rollback snapshot without creating a new data-loss risk.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, and utilitarian. Copy should state what will happen and what cannot be guaranteed without drama or decoration.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy time-travel themes, opaque one-click destructive actions, and a standalone visual system that conflicts with SillyBunny's native controls.

## Design Principles

- Put recoverability ahead of convenience.
- Show the exact target, version, and consequences before destructive work.
- Reuse SillyBunny's controls and visual language.
- Fail closed when a restore cannot be represented safely.
- Keep routine history management quiet and compact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard and screen-reader use, narrow mobile viewports, and status messaging that does not rely on color alone. Preserve native focus behavior and reduced-motion preferences.
